# MABOLO Animated Pilot — Keeladi: Ancient Tamil Life

**Target:** 36 seconds, vertical 9:16, stylized cinematic 3D animation inspired by the user's uploaded reference.  
**Production:** Free/local-first. No paid video generation or credit-consuming service is used.

## Visual direction
- Rounded expressive 3D characters, warm skin tones, period-inspired cotton clothing.
- Warm morning light, soft shadows, terracotta, wood, woven fibre and earth materials.
- Smooth camera moves, gestures and distinct shot changes.
- Artistic historical-life reconstruction only; not a literal reconstruction of a specific excavated house or a claim that depicted objects were found together at Keeladi.

## Storyboard
| Time | Shot | Action | Camera |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–8s | Dawn settlement | Residents begin the day; an adult and child walk past clay houses and pots | Slow push-in, wide shot |
| 8–17s | Potter's work | Potter shapes a clay vessel; child watches | Medium shot, gentle side move |
| 17–26s | Household craft | A woman arranges vessels and woven goods; a child helps | Close-to-medium tracking |
| 26–36s | Shared water | Residents gather at a water point; pots are filled | Slow pull-back to wide shot |

## Free workflow
1. Run the local Blender script at scripts/blender/keeladi_pilot.py.
2. Add Tamil narration and subtitles with the existing FFmpeg pipeline.
3. Review character consistency, frame quality, subtitle safe area and historical accuracy.
4. Only consider AI video tools later if the user explicitly approves any credit spend.

## Honest limitations
The first Blender pilot is a procedural 3D proof of concept. It has keyframed movement and camera cuts, but not advanced facial rigging, realistic walk cycles or lip-sync. It is not yet commercial AI-animation fidelity.
